SECTOR 00 — ABOUT US

About Urban Vision.

UrbanVision is a DST–NGP (Department of Science & Technology – National Geospatial Programme) sponsored research project hosted at Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH), focused on advancing automated and scalable Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) of bridges and urban infrastructure. The project integrates drone-based imaging, artificial intelligence, deep learning, LiDAR, multispectral sensing, and geospatial technologies to develop a comprehensive digital workflow for infrastructure inspection, analysis, reconstruction, and visualization.

At the data acquisition stage, UrbanVision employs a custom-built drone platform equipped with RGB, LiDAR, and multispectral sensors to capture high-resolution visual, three-dimensional, spectral, and geospatial information from infrastructure. The acquired datasets are processed using advanced computational resources, including High-Performance Computing (HPC) for AI model training on AIRAWAT, India’s national supercomputer, enabling the development of efficient deep-learning models for automated infrastructure analysis. The AI system is fine-tuned to identify eight major structural defect classes, transforming large volumes of drone imagery into meaningful and actionable inspection information.

Beyond automated analysis, UrbanVision focuses on 3D geospatial reconstruction by integrating RGB, LiDAR, and multispectral datasets using platforms such as Pix4Dmatic and Agisoft Metashape. These technologies enable the generation of detailed point clouds, 3D models, orthomosaics, and spatially accurate digital representations of bridges and urban structures. The reconstructed infrastructure can then be visualized and explored using Blender, Meta Quest 3 Virtual Reality, and an interactive Streamlit dashboard, providing an immersive and intuitive environment for understanding infrastructure conditions.

The ultimate goal of UrbanVision is to bridge the gap between advanced geospatial technologies and practical infrastructure management by delivering AI-assisted, spatially accurate, and visually intuitive insights to engineers, researchers, and urban planners without requiring extensive expertise in complex data-processing technologies. By combining intelligent drone-based sensing, deep learning, high-performance computing, 3D geospatial reconstruction, and immersive visualization, UrbanVision aims to contribute toward safer, smarter, scalable, and technology-driven infrastructure monitoring in India.

SECTOR 03 — SPECTRAL PHYSICS

RGB sees color.
Multispectral sees reason.

Criteria RGB alone Multispectral
Early rust / corrosion Reads as a brown stain only after paint fails; shadows confuse the model. An iron-oxide absorption dip near 650–700nm plus a NIR jump flags the chemical change before flaking.
Delamination / water ingress No visible cue until spalling or cracking appears. Water absorbs SWIR strongly — wet concrete reflects ~40% less, and thermal bands run 2–4°C cooler.
Crack vs. shadow vs. dirt All three read as dark pixels, driving high false-alarm rates. A real crack lowers NIR reflectance from cavity depth; shadow dims all bands equally; dirt shifts visible bands only.
Coating / paint failure Can't separate color fade from a lighting change. Pigment degradation shifts the spectral curve — blue drops first, catching failure before visible peeling.
Vegetation at joints Painted green and plant green look the same. NDVI = (NIR−Red)/(NIR+Red) > 0.3 flags live vegetation directly.
Cover detachment Reads as a dark line and little else. Exposed steel carries a distinct NIR/SWIR signature versus the surrounding concrete.
SECTOR 04 — HARDWARE INTEGRATION & DATA ACQUISITION

One airframe, six coordinated sensors.

Every sensor on the UAV reports to a single onboard compute stack, turning independent measurements into one synchronized capture pass.

The UAV integrates NVIDIA Jetson Orin, LiDAR, MicaSense RedEdge–P multispectral imaging, RGB imaging, RTK positioning, and DLS for coordinated data acquisition and processing. The sensors continuously collect 3D, spectral, visual, and positioning data, while the flight-control system uses real-time feedback to autonomously follow predefined inspection paths, maintain stability, and maneuver precisely around structures. Jetson Orin processes and fuses these datasets for accurate infrastructure inspection.

Jetson Orin

Onboard edge compute that fuses LiDAR, multispectral, RGB, and RTK streams into one dataset in real time.

RTK + DLS

RTK positioning delivers precise georeferencing while the Downwelling Light Sensor corrects multispectral readings for changing ambient light.

Autonomous flight control

Real-time feedback keeps the UAV on its predefined inspection path, stable and precisely maneuvered around structures.

SECTOR 05 — 2D ANOMALY DETECTION

Every defect, classified and located.

Each calibrated frame is scored per-pixel and grouped into two defect families, mirroring how field inspectors already log condition ratings.

Geometry anomalies

— LOSS OF FORM

Crack — linear fracture in concrete or coating, tracked and trended by width in millimeters.

Cover detachment — loss of bond between concrete cover and reinforcement; an early precursor to spalling.

Scaling — gradual loss of surface mortar that exposes coarse aggregate.

Spalling — a fragment of concrete breaking away, often exposing reinforcement beneath.

Void — a subsurface or surface cavity from poor consolidation or material erosion.

Appearance anomalies

— SURFACE CONDITION

Peeling — coating or paint separating and curling away from the substrate.

Efflorescence — white mineral salt deposits left as water migrates through concrete.

Corrosion — chemical degradation of embedded or exposed structural steel.

SECTOR 07 — WORKFLOW

Flight to report, in five stages.

The order matters — each stage feeds data the next stage depends on.

01

Capture

A UAV equipped with LiDAR, RGB, and Red Edge multispectral sensors surveys the structure, capturing high-resolution spatial and spectral data for comprehensive 3D mapping and structural assessment.

Input: flight plan, sensor rig
02

Fuse & Calibrate

LiDAR depth and RGB imagery are aligned using surveyed Ground Control Points for absolute georeferencing accuracy.

Output: co-registered dataset
03

Detect

Deep Learning models trained on the AIRAWAT supercomputing cluster, classifies geometry and appearance anomalies on every frame.

Output: per-frame defect masks
04

Reconstruct

Pix4Dmatic generate high-resolution orthomosaics, dense point clouds, and digital surface models.

Output: orthomosaic + point cloud + DSM
05

Visualize & Report

A Streamlit dashboard, Blender 3D scenes, and Meta Quest 3 VR turn the defect map into a stakeholder-ready inspection report.

Output: structured condition report
